# Brand guidelines

- **Voice:** confident, data-literate, never hype.
- **Say:** "retention", "evidence", "ship with confidence".
- **Avoid:** "revolutionary", "game-changing", exclamation marks.
- **Primary colour:** #1B4DFF.
